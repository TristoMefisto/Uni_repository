The blocked SE is √(Σj SEj² (Nj/N)²): the variance of a weighted sum of independent block estimates. It falls below the unblocked SE when potential outcomes are similar within block and different across blocks. Use the shift slider to make the regions alike (shift −11 puts region B on top of region A) and the gain disappears.

With mA/8 = mB/6 the block-fixed-effects regression weights the blocks by Nj/N and is fine. With unequal treatment shares (the default, 2/8 vs 2/6) its weights become ∝ Nj pj(1−pj), so the coefficient's expectation drifts away from the ATE. Interacting D with the centred block dummy, or reweighting by 1/pj and 1/(1−pj), restores the Nj/N weights exactly.

The sampling distribution of the ATE estimator

Gerber & Green Table 2.1: 7 villages, budget share under a male (Y(0)) or female (Y(1)) village head. Randomize which m villages are treated, compute the difference in means, and repeat for every possible assignment. The spread of those estimates is the standard error.

  1. Each row is a unit with both potential outcomes written down, so the true ATE is known. In a real experiment only one column shows.
  2. Press Next assignment: one lottery, one estimate. Do it a few times and watch the estimate move.
  3. Press Enumerate: every possible assignment at once. The mean of the histogram is the ATE, its SD is the SE.

Four ways to make the true SE smaller, all visible here: more units (N), a balanced split (m = N/2), and less spread in Y(0) or in Y(1). Edit the table to make the villages more alike and watch the histogram tighten; move m away from N/2 and watch it widen.

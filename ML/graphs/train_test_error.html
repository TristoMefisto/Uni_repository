The training MSE can be driven towards zero by a flexible enough model, because the fit is allowed to memorise the sample. The test MSE cannot: once the model follows the noise, new points from the same f land far from the curve. Compare the three presets: with a near-linear f the best degree is 1 or 2; with a highly non-linear f the U bottoms out much further right.

Test data are rarely available in practice, which is why the test MSE is estimated by cross-validation instead.

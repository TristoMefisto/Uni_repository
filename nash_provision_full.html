Reaction functions & the Nash equilibrium
As each reference level varies, every tangency traces a straight line — the reaction functions:
g¹(g²) = (1−α)M¹ − α·g²
g²(g¹) = (1−α)M² − α·g¹
Both slope downward (slope −α): more from one means less from the other — the graphical signature of free-riding. Where they cross, each contribution is simultaneously a best response to the other: the Nash equilibrium (ĝ¹,ĝ²) = (1.00,1.00), total provision G = 2.00.
The two faded curves are the indifference curves through the equilibrium: 1's is tangent to the horizontal g²=ĝ² and 2's to the vertical g¹=ĝ¹, both at the crossing — that is why the point is a mutual best response. It sits below the Samuelson (Pareto-efficient) level: the public good is under-provided.
